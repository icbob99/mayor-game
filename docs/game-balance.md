##### Mayor Game - Balance and Card Rules
Source of truth for the shared base card effects: adopted round-2 win5 candidate on 2026-11-25, with manual school repair and burger corrections. Values are before difficulty modifiers, passive drift and Arcade escalation (`game_config`).

###### Rules
* `unlimited`: may appear repeatedly during a cycle.
* `once`: at most once per cycle.
* `twice`: at most twice per cycle.
* `follow-up`: never selected by normal random selection; triggered by another card.
* Choice numbering starts at 1.
* Monetary hints were removed from choice text; money is shown in the `Money` column.

###### Optimizer LOCK & Safety Corridors
* **Base card corridors (before difficulty modifiers):**
  * `Money`: `-500 000` to `+2 500 000` (round values only).
  * `Threat`: max `+22` (`+25` on `follow-up` cards).
  * `Rating`: min `-15` (`-18` on `yom_haatzmaut` choice 3).
  * `Wife`: min `-15`.
  * `Health`: min `-15`.
* **Sign & Zero LOCK:** optimizer may scale non-zero values inside corridors, but MUST NOT invert signs (`+` / `-`) or change strict `0` values.
* **`FIX` status:** manual author overrides — completely frozen; optimizer MUST NOT alter any value in a `FIX` row.
