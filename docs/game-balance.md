### Mayor Game - Balance and Card Rules
Source of truth for the shared author-approved card effects from seed 029, restored by seed 033 (2026-11-23). Automated calibration has been rejected.
Values are before difficulty modifiers, passive drift and Arcade escalation. Normal starts at 90 health and uses x1.0; Easy gains/losses x1.2/x0.8, Hard x0.8/x1.3, Insane x0.6/x1.6. Author effects are restored exactly, without calibration or numerical caps. The first three completed decisions cannot cause stat defeat; Blitz timeout and voluntary resignation remain available.
#### Rules
*  unlimited: may appear repeatedly during a cycle.
*  once: at most once per cycle.
*  twice: at most twice per cycle.
*  follow-up: never selected by normal random selection; triggered by another card.
*  Choice numbering starts at 1.
*  Monetary hints were removed from choice text; money is shown in the Money column.
